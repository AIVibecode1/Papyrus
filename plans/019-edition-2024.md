## Why this matters

The crate uses `edition = "2021"`; edition 2024 has been stable since
Rust 1.85 (Feb 2025) and is what `cargo new` emits today. Nothing breaks
under 2021, but after the first commit an edition bump becomes a noisy
whole-crate diff. Today it's one line plus a compile — the cheapest
possible moment. Known edition-2024 gotchas (unsafe attribute syntax,
RPIT lifetime capture) don't appear in this codebase.
